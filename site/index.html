ORBIOA VIRTUAL ORGANOID FOR MICROGRAVITY

Rehearse the experiment before you fly it.

Flights to orbit are rare, costly and small. ORBIO simulates how a 3D mini-tissue grows and responds to drugs in microgravity, so you can compare designs on the ground, pick the ones worth flying, and keep watch once they are up there.

Why a sweet potato

It grows underground, where nobody can watch it. You tend it, you wait, and you only find out what happened when you dig it up.

That is still what flying biology to orbit feels like. You seal the hardware, it leaves, and months later you open it and read the result. ORBIO exists to shorten that blind stretch: to know a little more about what is happening before the digging.

ORBIO is a research prototype. Simulator outcomes only, with no wet-lab validation yet.
